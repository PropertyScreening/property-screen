GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Understand what a property screening visit can—and cannot—do.

Is PROPERTY SCREEN a 24/7 security service?+

No. It is designed as a periodic, scheduled, on-ground visual screening service. It does not provide continuous surveillance, guarding or emergency response.

Can you check vacant plots and land?+

That is a key use case. Share the location and access details so the team can confirm whether a visit can be arranged and what can be observed safely and lawfully.

Will I receive photographs or a visit report?+

The agreed service deliverables will be confirmed before onboarding. The proposed model is to provide visit observations with visual documentation where access, conditions and the agreed scope permit.

Does a screening visit verify ownership or legal boundaries?+

No. A visual screening visit is not title verification, a legal opinion, a cadastral or boundary survey, or a substitute for a licensed professional inspection.
